Introduction

Rationale

Partners need a pass product that they control. They decide who gets a pass, for what price, and for how long. The platform stores only what it needs to show a holding, check it at a site, and put it in a wallet.

Proposed Design

Summary. Three new tables: passes and two joins for partner sites and users. Each pass has one partner app, the owner. It manages the pass with an API key and is the only app that issues it. A site joins only when site staff opt it in, with the owner app's staff JWT. Only the owner issues and revokes holdings. A holding has no expiry: the app manages the term. After commit, a job adds or removes a wallet card for each holding. Issue is idempotent on the partner's external_id. Reads use the read replica.

      Rules
      • A partner app owns the pass (owner_app_id). It manages the pass with its API key and is the only issuer. It cannot add a site.
      • Staff opt in a site where they work, through the owner app's staff JWT. Any other app's token, or another site: 404. Staff or the owner app can remove a site.
      • Only the owner issues and revokes. Any other app gets 404. A composite key to passes (pass_id, owner_app_id) makes the database refuse a holding from another app.
      • Valid = revoked_at is null. A holding has no expiry: the app manages the term and revokes. No worker.
      • The owner app can read its holders' names on issue, on the holdings list, and on revoke.
      • Issue and revoke make no network call in the transaction. Wallet work runs after commit. A failed wallet call sets FAILED, keeps pending_operation, alerts, and retries. The holding stays valid.
      • Catch the unique violation on (app_id, external_id). Do not use ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING: it burns sequence values.
      • All reads run on the read replica, including the staff site check. Use one clock helper for "now".
