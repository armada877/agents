      Rules

      • Refresh processes feeds in sequence. A feed failure does not undo earlier successful updates.
      • Metadata-pending feeds omit validators from the request copy until a full refresh succeeds.
      • HTTP 304 updates feed validators and fetch time. It does not update articles or clear metadata_pending.
      • Article upserts preserve read state. Missing publication dates preserve an existing date.
      • Article keys use GUID, then link, then a hash. Duplicate keys in one response are skipped.
      • CLI article show preserves read state. Enter in the reader marks the article read.
